## 1. PURPOSE / OBJECTIVE

This One Point Lesson explains **psv-8901 testing & car-seal management** for FA-8901 (REFLUX ACCUMULATOR DRUM) in the HEXANE COLUMN OVERHEAD SYSTEM. The relief valve is the drum's last line of overpressure defence and must be proven and never isolated in service. The lesson gives operators and technicians the key knowledge and the correct step-by-step method so the task is done safely, correctly and consistently every time.

## 2. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

Hazard note: this equipment is **HIGH CRITICAL** handling **Hexane / water** at up to 10 barg / FV / 120 degC. Observe the following before and during the task:

- Apply LOTO (Lock-Out Tag-Out) and prove zero energy before starting work.
- Isolate, depressurise, drain and purge the equipment; obtain a valid work permit.
- Wear the correct PPE: coverall, safety shoes, gloves, goggles and hearing protection.
- Beware of stored mechanical energy (springs, rotating inertia) and pinch points.
- Use certified lifting gear and correct manual-handling technique for heavy parts.

## 3. TOOLS & MATERIALS REQUIRED

- Standard mechanical hand tools & calibrated torque wrench
- LOTO padlocks and permit
- Laser/dial alignment kit (as applicable)
- Feeler gauges, dial indicator, seal/bearing pullers
- Correct spare parts and gaskets per the BOM
- Lifting gear and slings (certified)

## 4. DETAILED PROCEDURE / STEPS

| Step | Action                                                     | Check / Acceptance          |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1    | Confirm PSV-8901 is set to 10 barg                         | Isolation & LOTO verified   |
| 2    | Carry out the off-line pop test per the statutory schedule | Parts match BOM / spec      |
| 3    | Keep the PSV isolation car-sealed open in service          | Torque / clearance to spec  |
| 4    | Tag and file the test certificate                          | No leak / smooth operation  |
| 5    | Never block-in a live PSV without an authorised spare      | Record in equipment history |
