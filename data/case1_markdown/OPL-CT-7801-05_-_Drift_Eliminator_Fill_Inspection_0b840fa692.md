## 1. PURPOSE / OBJECTIVE

This One Point Lesson explains **drift eliminator & fill inspection** for CT-7801 (COOLING TOWER CELL FAN) in the COOLING TOWER SYSTEM. Fouled or collapsed fill and drift eliminators cut the cooling performance and raise the basin temperature. The lesson gives operators and technicians the key knowledge and the correct step-by-step method so the task is done safely, correctly and consistently every time.

## 2. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

Hazard note: this equipment is **NON CRITICAL** handling **Circulating water / air** at up to atm / 50 degC. Observe the following before and during the task:

- Apply LOTO (Lock-Out Tag-Out) and prove zero energy before starting work.
- Isolate, depressurise, drain and purge the equipment; obtain a valid work permit.
- Wear the correct PPE: coverall, safety shoes, gloves, goggles and hearing protection.
- Beware of stored mechanical energy (springs, rotating inertia) and pinch points.
- Use certified lifting gear and correct manual-handling technique for heavy parts.

## 3. TOOLS & MATERIALS REQUIRED

- Standard mechanical hand tools & calibrated torque wrench
- LOTO padlocks and permit
- Laser/dial alignment kit (as applicable)
- Feeler gauges, dial indicator, seal/bearing pullers
- Correct spare parts and gaskets per the BOM
- Lifting gear and slings (certified)

## 4. DETAILED PROCEDURE / STEPS

| Step | Action                                             | Check / Acceptance          |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1    | Inspect the PVC fill for scaling and collapse      | Isolation & LOTO verified   |
| 2    | Check the drift eliminator packs are aligned       | Parts match BOM / spec      |
| 3    | Hose down fouled sections during a cell outage     | Torque / clearance to spec  |
| 4    | Report a thermal performance drop via TT-7801      | No leak / smooth operation  |
| 5    | Plan fill replacement if degradation is widespread | Record in equipment history |

## 6. KEY LEARNING POINTS

- Fouled or collapsed fill and drift eliminators cut the cooling performance and raise the basin temperature.
- Respect the interlock SEQ-7801: e.g. VSHH-7802 trips at > 9 mm/s - never defeat it without a permit.
- Always cross-check the Datasheet limits and the P&ID; TJC-LLD-PID-7801 before working on CT-7801.
- Record the work in the equipment history so the Knowledge Hub stays the single source of truth.
